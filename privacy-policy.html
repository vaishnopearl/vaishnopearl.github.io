Privacy Policy

Vaishno Pearl Maintenance Tracker (Android package com.manasatech.vaishnopearl) · Effective 2 October 2026

1. Who we are

The Vaishno Pearl Maintenance Tracker app ("the App") is used by Vaishno Pearl Flat Owners Mutually Aided Co-operative Society, Kompally, Hyderabad, Telangana ("the Society", "we") to manage maintenance dues, receipts, expenses, work orders and notices for its 20 flats. The App was developed for the Society by ManasaTech Innovations, Hyderabad.

The App is meant only for the Society's flat owners, residents and board members. It is not a public service and does not show advertising.

2. Information the App collects

WhatWhy it is used
Name, flat number, phone number, email addressTo identify flat owners and board members, send receipts and reminders, and contact residents about society matters.
Login details (user ID and password)To sign you in. Passwords are stored only in hashed (SHA-256) form, never as plain text.
Maintenance payments, dues, penalties, receipts and UPI/bank payment referencesTo keep the Society's accounts, issue receipts and prepare financial reports. The App does not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords. UPI payments are completed in your own UPI app.
Vehicle details (for parking stickers)To issue parking stickers and manage society parking.
Photos and documents you add (bills, work photos, notices)To record expenses and maintenance work.
Approximate device location, only when capturing a work photoTo confirm that maintenance work photos were taken at the society premises. Location is not tracked in the background or at any other time.
Notification tokenTo send you society notices and payment reminders, if you allow notifications.

Fingerprint / face login: if a board member turns on biometric login, the fingerprint or face check is done entirely by your phone. The App never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.

3. Where the information is stored

  • On your device – some data is kept in the App's local storage so it works offline.
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google, to sync data securely between board members' and residents' devices. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. See Firebase privacy information.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only:

  • with the Society's board members and authorised office bearers, for running the Society;
  • with Google Firebase, as the service provider that stores and syncs the data;
  • when you choose to share a receipt, report or notice through WhatsApp, email or another app – that sharing is done by you, through that app;
  • with auditors, tax authorities or other government bodies when required by law (for example, for the Society's audit and income-tax filings).

5. How long we keep it

Account and payment records are kept while you are a member or resident of the Society and afterwards for as long as the Society must keep its accounts under co-operative society and tax rules. Work photos and other records are deleted when they are no longer needed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or update your information at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and personal data. Write to the email below from your registered email or phone, mentioning your flat number. We will delete your login and personal details within 30 days, except records the Society must keep by law (such as payment receipts and audited accounts).
  • You can turn off location, camera or notification permissions in your phone settings at any time. Some features (such as work-photo upload) may then not work.

We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

7. Security

We use password hashing, role-based access (board members and flat owners see different data), encrypted connections and Firebase security rules to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

8. Children

The App is for flat owners and adult residents. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be announced in the App or on the Society notice board.
